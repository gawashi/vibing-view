<objective>
Make the W and M buttons functional by deriving weekly and monthly candles from cached daily bars — with zero additional FMP requests. Add a pure `aggregate.ts` (Monday-start weeks, calendar months, America/New_York boundaries, OHLCV bucket rules) and short-circuit `'1w'`/`'1M'` in `CacheService` so they read the daily bars and aggregate on the fly, never touching the provider.

Purpose: Second vertical slice of the phase story — after this, a user gains W/M resolution on top of the intraday/daily switching from Plan 01. It directly implements the API-saving invariant D-17 (the single most load-bearing decision of this phase).
Output: Working W and M candles derived from the daily cache.
